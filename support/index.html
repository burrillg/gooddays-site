Support

Email burrillgabriel@gmail.com with any question about Good Days. You'll usually hear back within two working days.

Subscriptions

Cancel

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so they're cancelled on your iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions › Good Days Pro › Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel it.

Restore

On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, sign in and tap Restore on the plans screen. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Refunds

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your account

Forgotten password

On the log-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder or email us.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Settings › Delete account. If your subscription has ended, Delete account is also on the plans screen. This deletes your account and everything in it straight away, and it can't be undone. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

AI logging

When you type, say or photograph what you ate, or describe a workout, Good Days asks once before sending it to OpenAI to work out the calories. You can turn this off at any time in Settings › Logging with OpenAI. Barcode scanning and manual entries work either way.
