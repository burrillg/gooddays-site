Good Days

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

In short:

  • We use what you tell Good Days to run your plan, and nothing else.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps.
  • Food you type, say or photograph goes to OpenAI to work out the calories, only after you allow it.
  • Apple Health data is only read, never written, and never sent to AI.
  • You can delete your account and its data in the app at any time.

1. Who we are

Good Days - Weight Loss Habits ("Good Days", "we", "us") is made by Gabriel Burrill, a sole trader in the United Kingdom. We are the controller of your personal data under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. Contact us at burrillgabriel@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Your account

Your email address and password. Passwords are stored by our sign-in provider in hashed form; we never see them.

Your plan

The details you give when you set up and use your plan: sex, date of birth, height, current and goal weight, activity level, step and exercise goals, units, time zone, your chosen coach and whether notifications are on. From these we work out your calorie and protein targets. If you add a starting photo, we keep that too.

What you log

  • Weigh-ins: weight and time.
  • Food and drink: what you ate, portions and nutrition, barcodes you scan, photos you take of meals and labels (kept with the entry), and the messages in your food chats.
  • Voice: when you say what you ate, the recording is sent to be turned into text and is then deleted from your phone. We keep the text, never the recording.
  • Exercise: workouts you log or import, and your daily steps.

Apple Health and your iPhone's motion sensor

If you connect Apple Health, Good Days reads your steps, walking and running distance, active energy and workouts. We store your daily step totals and imported workouts in your account so your plan and history work across sessions. We never write to Apple Health. If you choose your iPhone's step counter instead, we read step counts from its motion sensor. Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never sent to an AI service.

Subscriptions

Whether you have an active subscription or trial, and its purchase records. Payments are handled by Apple; we never see your card details.

Usage and technical data

Which screens you open and which features you use, linked to a random account ID rather than your name or email, and never including your weight, food or other health values. Our analytics provider may also record your device model, operating system, app version and an approximate location (country or city) worked out from your IP address. Our servers keep standard security logs, such as IP addresses and times of requests, and a record of each AI request's timing and outcome (not its content).

We don't collect your precise location, contacts or advertising identifier.

3. How we use it

  • To create your plan, show your targets and progress, and store your logs.
  • To estimate calories and nutrition from what you type, say or photograph, if you allow it (section 5).
  • To sell and manage your subscription, and to remind you before a free trial ends.
  • To understand how the app is used so we can fix problems and improve it.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent misuse.
  • To reply when you contact us.

4. Our legal bases

  • Contract: to provide the app you signed up for, including your account, plan, logs and subscription.
  • Explicit consent: for information about your health, such as weight, food and exercise, which you choose to give us, and for Apple Health. You can withdraw it by deleting that information, disconnecting Apple Health or deleting your account.
  • Consent: for sending what you log to OpenAI (section 5). You can withdraw it in Settings.
  • Legitimate interests: for analytics, security and fraud prevention, in ways you'd reasonably expect.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

5. AI food and exercise logging

When you type, say or photograph what you ate, or describe a workout, Good Days sends it to OpenAI to work out the calories and nutrition. The app asks for your permission the first time, and sends nothing if you say no. What's sent is the text, photo or voice recording itself, with no name, email or Apple Health data. OpenAI processes it on our behalf, doesn't use it to train its models, and may keep it for up to 30 days to detect abuse before deleting it. You can turn this off at any time in Settings › Logging with OpenAI; barcode scanning and manual entries keep working.

7. International transfers

Where a provider processes data outside the UK, for example in the United States, the transfer is protected by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses or by the UK-US data bridge.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account in the app, we delete your profile, plan, logs, photos, chats and sign-in straight away. Copies in our providers' encrypted backups are overwritten within 30 days. Analytics events stay with our analytics provider, linked only to a random ID that no longer connects to any account. Apple and RevenueCat keep purchase records as tax and accounting law requires. Deleting the app alone doesn't delete your account.

9. How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit. Each account can only reach its own data, photos are kept in private storage, and access to our systems is limited to what's needed to run the app.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, receive a copy in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. To use any of these rights, email burrillgabriel@gmail.com; we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

11. Age

Good Days is for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If we learn that we have, we'll delete it.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app before it applies.
